# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

[English](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md) · [한국어](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ko.md) · [日本語](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md)

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues, and other contributions that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participants when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces. Examples of representing the project or community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to `support@1day1ott.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the safety and privacy of the reporter.

Depending on the nature and recurrence of the violation, enforcement may include a private warning, an official warning, a temporary restriction on participation, or a permanent restriction on participation.
